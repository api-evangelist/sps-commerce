# SPS Commerce

> SPS Commerce is the world’s most intelligent supply chain network, utilizing agentic AI and a multi-tenant architecture to transform global trading relationships. By processing over 750 million annual transactions, the SPS network provides automated compliance detection, proactive revenue recovery, and agentic reasoning to help 50,000+ customers scale with intelligence.

SupplierWiki is a free community knowledge base that helps suppliers learn, stay current, and improve execution across these workflows.

- [Analytics](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/analytics/): Retail sales and inventory analytics for suppliers that need insight into product performance, sell-through, inventory, and channel trends.
- [Assortment](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/assortment/): Product information management solution for sharing complete, accurate item data with retailers and sales channels.
- [E-Invoicing](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/e-invoicing/): E-invoicing solution for exchanging compliant invoice documents without manually managing varying trading partner, tax, and regulatory requirements.
- [Revenue Recovery](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/revenue-recovery/): Deduction management and revenue recovery solution for identifying, validating, disputing, and recovering revenue tied to deductions, shortages, fines, and margin loss.
- [SPS for 3PLs](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/sps-for-3pls/): Solution for third-party logistics providers to standardize and automate customer communications, item data, inventory, orders, delivery, and status information.
- [EDI Integrations](https://www.spscommerce.com/products/integrations/): Pre-built EDI integrations for ERP, WMS, accounting, and other business systems, including connections for common business platforms.
